## 1. Purpose

These Affiliate Terms describe the draft operating rules for an XIIGen affiliate, referral, partner, or marketplace-introduction program. The program, if launched, allows approved participants to refer eligible customers to XIIGen and potentially receive compensation under the approved commercial terms.

## 2. Relationship to Other XIIGen Terms

These Affiliate Terms must be read together with the XIIGen Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, Enterprise License Agreement, Marketplace Listing and Distribution Compliance Policy, Marketing and Public Claims Review Policy, and any approved written affiliate order form or partner addendum.

If these Affiliate Terms conflict with an executed written agreement signed by XIIGen, the signed agreement controls for that affiliate relationship.

## 3. Eligibility and Approval

Participation is not automatic. XIIGen may require each affiliate to complete onboarding, identity checks, tax forms, payment setup, marketplace compliance review, and approval of public claims before referral activity begins.

XIIGen may reject, suspend, or terminate participation for inaccurate claims, unsafe AI claims, privacy or security misstatements, brand misuse, suspected fraud, prohibited traffic, sanctions risk, or violation of applicable laws.

## 4. Approved Marketing Claims

Affiliates may only use product descriptions, screenshots, demos, links, discount language, and performance claims that are approved by XIIGen in writing. Affiliates must not state or imply that XIIGen produces guaranteed correct code, replaces developer review, replaces security review, provides legal advice, provides compliance certification, or makes production deployment decisions autonomously.

All public claims must preserve this positioning: XIIGen is a developer-assistance and code-context tool; outputs require human review, testing, validation, and security approval before production use.

## 5. Prohibited Conduct

Affiliates must not:

1. use misleading, deceptive, high-pressure, or unlawful marketing;
2. bid on protected XIIGen trademarks or confusingly similar terms without written approval;
3. send spam, unsolicited messages, or messages that violate consent or privacy rules;
4. publish fake reviews, fake demos, fake metrics, or unapproved benchmark claims;
5. represent themselves as XIIGen employees, legal representatives, auditors, or certification authorities;
6. target restricted jurisdictions, sanctioned parties, or prohibited use cases;
7. collect customer credentials, source code, secrets, payment data, or private project content except through approved XIIGen flows;
8. bypass marketplace, export-control, privacy, or security review requirements.

Referral attribution rules must be defined in the approved program configuration before launch. The final approved version must state the tracking method, attribution window, duplicate-referral handling, coupon or link rules, customer eligibility, and the record used as the source of truth for payment calculations.

## 8. Refunds, Cancellations, Chargebacks, and Fraud

XIIGen may reverse, withhold, or offset compensation for refunded purchases, cancelled subscriptions, chargebacks, duplicate accounts, self-referrals, fraudulent traffic, prohibited incentives, policy violations, or transactions that do not meet the approved eligibility criteria.

## 9. Customer Data and Privacy

Affiliates must process personal data only as permitted by applicable law and the approved affiliate program configuration. Affiliates must provide their own required privacy notices and must not collect or process customer data on XIIGen's behalf unless a written data processing arrangement applies.

Affiliates must not request source code, credentials, secrets, private database content, or confidential customer project files as part of referral activity.

## 10. Brand and Content Use

XIIGen may provide approved logos, links, screenshots, copy, or product descriptions. Affiliates may use only the approved materials and must stop using them when requested. Affiliates may not modify XIIGen marks, imply sponsorship beyond the approved affiliate relationship, or register domains, social accounts, ads, or marketplace listings that create confusion with XIIGen.

## 11. Compliance with Marketplaces and Laws

Affiliates are responsible for complying with advertising laws, consumer protection rules, privacy rules, tax obligations, anti-spam rules, marketplace requirements, sanctions/export controls, and any required affiliate disclosures. Example disclosures must be reviewed and approved before publication.

## 12. Termination

XIIGen may suspend or terminate an affiliate relationship or program access for any policy violation, legal risk, brand risk, suspected fraud, unavailable payment method, failed compliance review, or business reason stated in the approved final terms.
