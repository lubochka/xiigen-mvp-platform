# Code of Conduct

Communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code and observable behavior. Welcome questions and differing experience levels. Explain disagreements constructively and respect contributors' boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and deliberate sharing of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not publish credentials, private tenant data or personal details in project discussions, issues or pull requests.

Maintainers may ask participants to revise or remove inappropriate content and may limit participation when needed to protect the community.

For a conduct concern, follow the sensitive-contact procedure in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). The initial issue should only request a private channel; share identifying or sensitive details after the channel is agreed.
